Snake Shop

Earn 1 Snakebuck every 5 score. Higher levels unlock free passives automatically.
Some upgrades can be bought OR unlocked by reaching certain levels.
Longer Ghost – Ghost ability lasts longer. (Free at Lv 5) Cost: 15 SB
Slow Time+ – Slow Time lasts longer. (Free at Lv 7) Cost: 15 SB
Shockwave+ – Shockwave cooldown is shorter. (Free at Lv 9) Cost: 20 SB
Passive Income – Gain Snakebucks slowly over time. (Free at Lv 4) Cost: 25 SB
Ally+ – Stronger ally snake AI. (Free at Lv 6) Cost: 20 SB
Big Food – Food gives more score. (Free at Lv 3) Cost: 15 SB
Revive Token – One extra life per run. (Free at Lv 10) Cost: 40 SB
